# Delete your Pathfolio account or data

Pathfolio is developed by Kenny Lee. You can delete your Pathfolio cloud account, or the map on your phone, at any time.

## Delete your cloud account in the app

1. Open **Pathfolio** and sign in with Google.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account & cloud data** and confirm.

This deletes your account and everything backed up. The map on this phone is kept.

## Delete the map on this phone

Clear Pathfolio's app data in Android / iOS settings, or uninstall the app. That removes the local trail, pins and badges.

## Without the app

Email [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com) from the Google account you use with Pathfolio, with the subject "Delete my Pathfolio account". We act on the request within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email address and user ID), when you delete the account
- Cloud backup of visited cells
- Optional: local trail, pins and badges, if you clear app data or uninstall

## What may be kept

- Purchase records kept by Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat, as required for billing and tax
- Encrypted backups held by our database provider (Supabase), which expire within 30 days

## 刪除 Pathfolio 帳號或資料

- **刪除雲端帳號：** 設定 → 刪除帳號與雲端資料 → 確認。手機上的地圖會保留。
- **刪除手機地圖：** 在系統設定清除 App 資料，或解除安裝。
- **不用 App：** 用你登入 Pathfolio 的 Google 帳號寄信到 [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com)，主旨「Delete my Pathfolio account」。我們會在 30 天內處理並回信確認。
- **會刪除：** 登入帳號（刪除帳號時）、雲端足跡備份。
- **可能保留：** Google Play / App Store 與 RevenueCat 的購買記錄；Supabase 的加密備份會在 30 天內過期。
